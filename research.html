01 / Ongoing research

Urban canopy flows

Isothermal street canyon flow under tall building influence

How do tall buildings change the interaction between the atmospheric boundary layer and the streets below? This project investigates the influence of building height on turbulence and pollutant dispersion.

  • Precursor LES of a rough-wall atmospheric boundary layer using a drag-porosity canopy.
  • Successor LES of an idealised street canyon with a tall upstream building, driven by precursor inflow.
  • Building-height sensitivity on a 50-million-cell mesh, using 128–256 cores.
  • Passive-scalar tracer released at the canyon floor to study dispersion.
  • Validation against wind-tunnel Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV).

02 / Ongoing research

Floating offshore wind

Wake dynamics and reconstruction

This project combines large-eddy simulation with sensor-informed reconstruction to study the wakes of moving offshore wind turbines.

  • LES of wind turbine wakes using an actuator-line model with multiple degrees of freedom.
  • 1:75-scale DTU 10 MW model of the NETTUNO wind-tunnel tests.
  • Moving tower and nacelle represented by volume penalisation, without re-meshing.
  • Wake resolved to six rotor diameters on a 32-million-cell mesh and validated against measurements.
  • Plane-to-volume wake reconstruction from a single measured plane.

Experiments conducted in collaboration with LHEEA, École Centrale de Nantes, using models typically built at 1:200 scale. The generated boundary layer agrees well with ESDU guidelines.
