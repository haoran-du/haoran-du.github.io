Conference: PHYSMOD 2026, Potsdam, Germany · 25–28 August 2026.

Presentation: “Data-driven reconstruction of large scales in atmospheric boundary layer flow from sparse SPIV/HWA measurements.”

Conference version. These slides show the work presented at PHYSMOD 2026 and are not the final version of the study. For the final versions of the studies, please refer to the peer-reviewed journal articles.

The presentation explores how sparse measurements can recover large-scale flow structures. It compares linear estimation with neural-network approaches, using wind-tunnel data and reduced-order representations of the velocity field.
