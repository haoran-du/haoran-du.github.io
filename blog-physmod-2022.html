PHYSMOD 2022: An upstream tall building and street canyon flow

By Haoran Du · Published August 29, 2022

Slides from PHYSMOD 2022 on wind-tunnel measurements of how an upstream tall building affects turbulence in a street canyon.

Conference: PHYSMOD 2022, Prague, Czechia · 29–31 August 2022.

Presentation: “Effect of an upstream tall building on a street canyon flow.”

Conference version. These slides show the work presented at PHYSMOD 2022 and are not the final version of the study. For the final versions of the studies, please refer to the peer-reviewed journal articles.

The presentation describes wind-tunnel experiments with a model of Rue de Strasbourg. It examines how the height and geometry of an upstream tall building affect mean flow, turbulence statistics, and vertical exchange in the street canyon.
