Conference: PHYSMOD 2024, Lyon, France · 28–30 August 2024.

Presentation: “Interaction between the large scales in the boundary layer and street canyon flow.”

Conference version. These slides show the work presented at PHYSMOD 2024 and are not the final version of the study. For the final versions of the studies, please refer to the peer-reviewed journal articles.

This talk presented early work using proper orthogonal decomposition with linear stochastic estimation (POD-LSE) to reconstruct large-scale boundary-layer motions from limited measurements. It examined how these structures relate to flow and mass exchange in a street canyon. This work was a precursor to the 2026 Experiments in Fluids paper on reconstruction from sparse measurements.
