CFDSC / CSME 2025: Simulating street canyon flow

By Haoran Du · Published May 28, 2025

Slides from the 2025 congress on large-eddy simulations of street canyon flow with a drag-porosity modelled atmospheric boundary layer.

Conference: CFDSC / CSME 2025, Montréal, Canada · 25–28 May 2025.

Presentation: “Characteristics of street canyon flow using Large-Eddy Simulation with a drag-porosity modelled atmospheric boundary layer.”

Conference version. These slides show the work presented at CFDSC / CSME 2025 and are not the final version of the study. For the final versions of the studies, please refer to the peer-reviewed journal articles.

The presentation describes a drag-porosity method for generating the incoming urban boundary layer in OpenFOAM, followed by large-eddy simulations of street canyons with different aspect ratios. It presents the simulation setup and preliminary comparisons of the flow and turbulence statistics.
