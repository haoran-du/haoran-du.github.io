Presentation: PhD comprehensive exam, Western University · 23 February 2026.

Title: “Sensor-informed reduced-order modelling of street canyon flow and overlying atmospheric boundary layer flow.”

Exam version. These slides record the research plan and progress presented at my comprehensive exam. They include preliminary results and proposed work, and are not the final versions of the studies. For final published methods and findings, please refer to the peer-reviewed journal articles.

The presentation asks how sparse time-series measurements can be used to reconstruct urban velocity and pollutant fields. It outlines a research programme combining wind-tunnel measurements and large-eddy simulations with reduced-order representations and sensor-to-field models, with ventilation and dispersion as applications.

The deck includes the research objectives, the timeline and progress as presented at the exam, and supplementary slides.
